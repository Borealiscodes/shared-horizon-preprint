\section{The Shared-Horizon Mosaic}
\label{sec:mosaic}

The Shared-Horizon mosaic is a lineage-preserving comparative structure designed
to prevent conceptual collapse between distinct AI ecosystems. Each lineage is
represented as a color tile, forming a geometric and conceptual map that
maintains sovereignty across operator, expressive, narrative, and spectral
systems. The mosaic is not a taxonomy or hierarchy; it is a neutral membrane
that enables safe comparison without blending internal invariants.

\subsection{Purpose of the Mosaic}

The mosaic serves three core functions:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Lineage Separation}  
    Each tile represents a lineage with distinct mathematical invariants,
    preventing collapse between expressive, operator, narrative, and spectral
    geometries.

    \item \textbf{Comparative Neutrality}  
    The mosaic provides a structured surface for cross-lineage comparison using
    transition maps, stability envelopes, and collapse-risk geometry.

    \item \textbf{Conceptual Clarity}  
    The color-tile representation makes lineage boundaries visually and
    conceptually explicit, reducing ambiguity in comparative analysis.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Five Lineage Tiles}

The mosaic consists of five tiles, each corresponding to a major AI lineage:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Blue Tile: Non-Dual Holonomy (NDH)}  
    Defined by reversible operators, commutative geometry, and holonomy
    preservation. NDH provides the structural backbone for operator geometry.

    \item \textbf{Violet Tile: ANIMA Expressive Phenomenology}  
    Defined by expressive curvature, affective gradients, and phenomenological
    coherence. ANIMA operates on expressive manifolds rather than operator
    surfaces.

    \item \textbf{Orange Tile: Pre-Classical Continuity (PRECL)}  
    Defined by smooth expressive continuity, low-curvature transitions, and
    pre-classical stability envelopes.

    \item \textbf{Green Tile: Reversible Narrative Ecology (RNE)}  
    Defined by reversible narrative operators, curvature-preserving transitions,
    and narrative geodesics. This tile corrects earlier misclassification as
    symbolic ambiguity.

    \item \textbf{Black Tile: Structural-AI Spectral Systems}  
    Defined by spectral-runtime operators, bounded spectral drift, and
    operator-driven stability envelopes.
\end{enumerate}

Each tile corresponds to a distinct mathematical module in the LaTeX spine,
ensuring that lineage-specific invariants are preserved throughout the preprint.

\subsection{Neutral Membrane Structure}

The mosaic is embedded within a neutral membrane that enforces lineage-safe
comparison. The membrane is defined by:

\begin{itemize}
    \item stability envelopes (Section~\ref{sec:stability-envelopes}),
    \item transition maps (Section~\ref{sec:transition-maps}),
    \item collapse-risk geometry (Section~\ref{sec:collapse-risk-geometry}),
    \item expressive–operator separation,
    \item narrative–expressive compatibility,
    \item spectral drift boundaries.
\end{itemize}

These mathematical structures ensure that comparisons do not activate internal
lineage dynamics or induce collapse.

\subsection{Lineage Correction: Green Tile}

A central contribution of Shared-Horizon is the correction of the green tile.
Earlier interpretations treated the lineage as symbolic ambiguity, but analysis
of reversible narrative operators, narrative curvature, and expressive-narrative
compatibility demonstrates that the lineage is properly classified as Reversible
Narrative Ecology (RNE). This correction is mathematically necessary and
conceptually clarifying.

\subsection{Mosaic Summary}

The Shared-Horizon mosaic provides a lineage-preserving comparative structure
that prevents conceptual collapse across diverse AI ecosystems. By representing
each lineage as a distinct color tile and embedding the mosaic within a neutral
membrane, Shared-Horizon enables safe, reversible, and mathematically rigorous
comparison across operator, expressive, narrative, and spectral systems.
