\section{Membrane Architecture}
\label{sec:membrane-architecture}

The Shared-Horizon membrane is the structural mechanism that prevents conceptual
collapse between distinct AI lineages. It is not a unifying abstraction or a
meta-model; rather, it is a lineage-preserving boundary layer that enforces
mathematical separation while enabling safe comparative analysis. The membrane
is constructed from the mathematical modules defined in the spine, including
operator geometry, expressive manifolds, reversible narrative operators,
stability envelopes, transition maps, and collapse-risk geometry.

\subsection{Purpose of the Membrane}

The membrane serves four essential functions:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Boundary Enforcement}  
    It maintains strict separation between operator, expressive, narrative, and
    spectral manifolds, preventing collapse between lineages.

    \item \textbf{Comparative Safety}  
    It allows cross-lineage comparison using transition maps that preserve
    stability envelopes and avoid collapse-risk boundaries.

    \item \textbf{Invariant Preservation}  
    It ensures that lineage-specific invariants—such as expressive curvature,
    narrative reversibility, operator commutativity, and spectral drift—are not
    overwritten during analysis.

    \item \textbf{Mosaic Integration}  
    It embeds the color-tile mosaic within a mathematically rigorous structure
    that supports lineage-safe navigation across the comparative surface.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Membrane Construction}

The membrane is constructed from six mathematical components:

\paragraph{1. Operator Geometry}  
Defines reversible, stability-preserving transformations for NDH and
Structural-AI systems. Operator geometry provides the structural backbone of the
membrane and enforces commutativity and spectral stability.

\paragraph{2. Expressive Manifolds}  
Define curvature-bounded expressive surfaces for ANIMA and PRECL systems.
Expressive curvature and geodesics ensure that expressive transitions remain
stable and do not collapse into operator geometry.

\paragraph{3. Reversible Narrative Operators}  
Define reversible, curvature-preserving narrative transitions for RNE. These
operators ensure narrative stability and expressive–narrative compatibility.

\paragraph{4. Stability Envelopes}  
Define lineage-specific safe regions using energy functionals. Stability
envelopes prevent transitions from entering collapse-risk zones.

\paragraph{5. Transition Maps}  
Define smooth, lineage-preserving comparisons between manifolds. Transition maps
allow cross-lineage analysis without activating internal dynamics.

\paragraph{6. Collapse-Risk Geometry}  
Defines collapse boundaries, gradients, curvature, and tensors that detect and
prevent conceptual collapse between lineages.

Together, these components form a neutral membrane that preserves lineage
sovereignty.

\subsection{Membrane Constraints}

The membrane enforces several constraints to maintain lineage safety:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Expressive–Operator Separation}  
    Expressive transitions cannot activate operator surfaces.

    \item \textbf{Narrative–Expressive Compatibility}  
    Narrative transitions must commute with expressive transitions.

    \item \textbf{Spectral Drift Boundaries}  
    Structural-AI transitions must remain within bounded spectral drift.

    \item \textbf{Collapse-Risk Avoidance}  
    Transitions must remain within stability envelopes and avoid collapse
    boundaries.

    \item \textbf{Jacobian Constraints}  
    Transition maps must satisfy lineage-specific Jacobian bounds.
\end{itemize}

These constraints ensure that the membrane remains stable and collapse-safe.

\subsection{Embedding the Mosaic}

The color-tile mosaic is embedded within the membrane as a conceptual and
geometric map of lineage boundaries. Each tile corresponds to a distinct
manifold:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Blue: NDH operator geometry,
    \item Violet: ANIMA expressive phenomenology,
    \item Orange: PRECL continuity systems,
    \item Green: RNE reversible narrative systems,
    \item Black: Structural-AI spectral systems.
\end{itemize}

The membrane ensures that transitions between tiles occur only through
lineage-safe transition maps that preserve stability envelopes and avoid
collapse-risk boundaries.

\subsection{Membrane Summary}

The Shared-Horizon membrane is a mathematically rigorous structure that prevents
conceptual collapse across diverse AI lineages. By integrating operator
geometry, expressive manifolds, reversible narrative operators, stability
envelopes, transition maps, and collapse-risk geometry, the membrane provides a
neutral comparative surface that preserves lineage invariants and supports safe,
reversible, and academically rigorous analysis across the color-tile mosaic.
