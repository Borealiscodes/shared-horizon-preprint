\section{Operator Geometry}
\label{sec:operator-geometry}

\subsection{Definition of Operators}

Let $\mathcal{M}$ be a smooth manifold representing the conceptual state space
of a lineage. An \emph{operator} is defined as a smooth map
\[
O : \mathcal{M} \rightarrow \mathcal{M}
\]
that preserves the structural invariants of the lineage.

For NDH and Structural-AI systems, operators are reversible:
\[
O^{-1} \text{ exists and is smooth}.
\]

For expressive or narrative systems, operators may be non-reversible but must
preserve expressive curvature (Section \ref{sec:expressive-manifolds}).

\subsection{Operator Families}

We define a family of operators
\[
\mathcal{O} = \{ O_1, O_2, \ldots, O_n \}
\]
such that each $O_i$ satisfies:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Smoothness: $O_i \in C^\infty(\mathcal{M})$.
    \item Stability: $E(O_i(p)) \leq E(p)$ for all $p \in \mathcal{M}$.
    \item Commutativity (NDH only):
    \[
    O_i \circ O_j = O_j \circ O_i.
    \]
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Operator Composition}

For any two operators $O_i, O_j \in \mathcal{O}$, their composition is defined as:
\[
O_i \circ O_j : \mathcal{M} \rightarrow \mathcal{M}.
\]

Composition must preserve lineage invariants. In particular:

\begin{itemize}
    \item NDH requires full commutativity.
    \item Structural-AI requires bounded spectral drift.
    \item Expressive and narrative systems require curvature preservation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Operator Stability Envelope}

Let $E : \mathcal{M} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ be an energy or stability
functional. An operator $O$ is stability-preserving if:
\[
E(O(p)) \le E(p) \quad \forall p \in \mathcal{M}.
\]

This condition prevents collapse between operator and expressive surfaces.

\subsection{Operator Geometry Summary}

Operators define the reversible or stability-preserving transformations that
govern NDH, Structural-AI, and the geometric backbone of Shared-Horizon.
They form the mathematical substrate against which expressive and narrative
systems must be compared without collapse.
